# JointTrack — Disclaimer and Scope of Use

**Version 1.0 | Last updated: 2024**

---

## This software is not a medical device.

JointTrack is a wellness and personal documentation application. It records:

- Objective kinematic measurements (joint angles computed from camera landmarks)
- Completed patient-reported outcome questionnaire responses (Oxford Knee Score / Oxford Hip Score)
- Longitudinal summaries of the above measurements over time

**It does not diagnose medical conditions. It does not recommend treatments. It does not replace professional clinical assessment.**

---

## Intended Use

JointTrack is intended to help adults—particularly those aged 60+ in Ireland who are waiting for physiotherapy or specialist orthopaedic review—to:

1. Keep a consistent, objective record of their lower-limb mobility over time
2. Complete validated self-report questionnaires at regular intervals
3. Generate a clear summary document to share with a GP, physiotherapist, or orthopaedic consultant at their next appointment

---

## What This Application Is Not

| This app is NOT | Because |
|-----------------|---------|
| A diagnostic tool | It does not interpret measurements against diagnostic criteria |
| A triage system | It does not prioritise clinical urgency |
| A substitute for seeing a doctor | No software can replace in-person clinical examination |
| An EU Medical Device (MDR Class I–III) | It is intentionally scoped as wellness/documentation software |
| Clinically validated for accuracy | The angle measurements are indicative only; a trained physiotherapist using a calibrated goniometer remains the clinical standard |

---

## Measurement Accuracy

Camera-based pose estimation (MediaPipe Pose Landmarker) provides useful trend data but is subject to:

- Lighting conditions
- Clothing (loose fabric obscures body landmarks)
- Camera angle and distance
- Subject positioning
- Device processing power

**Treat all angle values as relative indicators of change over time, not as absolute clinical measurements.**

---

## Red-Flag Warning — Seek Immediate Medical Attention If You Experience:

- Sudden, severe pain significantly worse than your usual joint pain
- Inability to bear weight on the affected leg
- Locking or catching of the joint (inability to straighten or bend)
- Fever alongside joint pain or swelling (possible sign of infection or inflammatory arthritis flare)
- Sudden, rapid joint swelling (particularly after a fall or impact)
- Numbness or tingling in the leg or foot
- Signs of blood clot (calf pain, redness, swelling, or warmth)

**Stop using this application and contact your GP, an emergency department, or call 999/112 immediately if any of the above apply.**

---

## Data and Privacy

- All measurement data is stored locally on your device in browser storage (IndexedDB).
- No video footage is transmitted, uploaded, or stored.
- No data is sent to any server.
- You can export all your data as a JSON file at any time.
- You can delete all your data from the app settings at any time.

The developers of JointTrack have no access to your personal data.

## Regulatory Status

JointTrack is designed and scoped as wellness/documentation software outside the definition of a medical device under EU MDR 2017/745 Article 2(1). It does not perform diagnosis, prognosis, monitoring for decision-making in treatment, or physiological control. If you are a healthcare innovator adapting this codebase for a purpose that may cross into clinical decision support or diagnosis, you are responsible for conducting an appropriate regulatory assessment.
